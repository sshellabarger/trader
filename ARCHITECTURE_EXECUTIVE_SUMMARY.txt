================================================================================
TRADING SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE - EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
================================================================================

Your trading system has THREE core architectural layers:

1. STRATEGIES (10 scoring functions)
   Purpose: "Should I enter this symbol?"
   Example: Momentum=0.65, News=0.45, Volume=0.70 → Combined=0.62 → ENTRY
   File: src/trading_bot/strategies.py
   
2. REGIMES (6 market classifications)
   Purpose: "What type of market is this?"
   Types: TRENDING_UP, TRENDING_DOWN, RANGING, HIGH_VOLATILITY, LOW_VOLATILITY
   Effect: Determines which strategies matter (weights: 0.02-0.40)
   File: src/trading_bot/strategy_manager.py:16-105
   
3. MULTIPLIERS (risk adjustment factors)
   Purpose: "Adjust risk parameters for this market"
   Current: Only applied to stop-loss width (0.7× to 3.0×)
   File: src/trading_bot/strategy_manager.py:700-736

================================================================================
THE CONCEPTUAL PROBLEM
================================================================================

STRATEGIES answer: "Is the market trending up right now?"
REGIMES answer:   "Are we in a TRENDING_UP market?"
MULTIPLIERS:      "Adjust stops for this market type"

But NEWS/EARNINGS are fundamentally DIFFERENT:
- Not about "current market state" → Event-based (forward-looking)
- Not about "price action" → Scheduled (known dates)
- Not about "entry opportunity" → Risk-management critical
- Current approach: Weak scoring signal
- Better approach: Market regime override + risk adjusters

Example Problem:
  Day 1: Earnings in 1 day
  Current system: Earnings score = 0.86 (weight = 0.02) → 1.7% contribution
  Proposed system: EARNINGS_IMMINENT regime → Entry threshold +0.10, stops ×1.8, 
                   position size ×0.6, close before announcement

NEWS & EARNINGS CURRENT BEHAVIOR
================================================================================

NEWS SCORING (strategies.py:93-181)
  Input: List[NewsArticle] with sentiment_score (-1 to +1)
  Calculation:
    volume_score = log(count+1) / log(20) × 0.5  → 0 to 0.5 range
    sentiment_component = avg_sentiment × 0.5  → -0.5 to +0.5 range
    final_score = volume_score + sentiment_component  → 0 to 1.0
  
  Regime Weights:
    TRENDING_UP: 0.07 (low)
    RANGING: 0.15 (highest)
    HIGH_VOLATILITY: 0.13
    LOW_VOLATILITY: 0.10
  
  Example: 8 articles, +0.5 sentiment
    volume_score = log(9)/log(20) × 0.5 ≈ 0.33
    sentiment = +0.5 × 0.5 = +0.25
    final = 0.58
    weighted = 0.58 × 0.07 to 0.15 = 0.04-0.09 contribution to entry
    → Too weak!

EARNINGS SCORING (strategies.py:208-230)
  Input: earnings_calendar[symbol]['days_until']
  Calculation:
    if days_until <= 7:
      score = 1.0 - (days_until / 7)
    else:
      score = 0
  
  Scoring:
    7+ days: 0.00 (nothing)
    6 days: 0.14
    4 days: 0.43
    2 days: 0.71
    1 day: 0.86
    0 days: 1.00
  
  Regime Weights:
    TRENDING_UP: 0.02 (LOWEST!)
    RANGING: 0.04
    HIGH_VOLATILITY: 0.04
    LOW_VOLATILITY: 0.05
  
  Example: 1 day before earnings
    score = 0.86
    weighted = 0.86 × 0.02 to 0.05 = 0.017-0.043
    → Only 1-4% of final entry decision!
    → No position sizing adjustment
    → No stop loss widening
    → No early exit mechanism

CURRENT REGIME MULTIPLIERS (Stop Loss Only)
================================================================================

Applied in: strategy_manager.py:calculate_stop_loss()

Base stop loss: 50 basis points (0.5%)

Multipliers by regime:
  TRENDING_UP: 0.8x  → 40 bps (tighter in trends)
  TRENDING_DOWN: 1.2x → 60 bps
  RANGING: 1.0x → 50 bps (normal)
  HIGH_VOLATILITY: 1.5x → 75 bps (wider for swings)
  LOW_VOLATILITY: 0.7x → 35 bps (tighter)
  Crypto: ×2.0 (additional multiplier)

Example: HIGH_VOLATILITY + Crypto
  50 × 1.5 × 2.0 = 150 bps = 1.5% stop
  (vs normal 0.5%)

================================================================================
RECOMMENDATION: EVENT-BASED REGIMES
================================================================================

Instead of treating News/Earnings as scoring strategies, create separate
EVENT REGIMES that apply comprehensive multipliers:

NEW EVENT REGIMES:
  EARNINGS_IMMINENT (0-2 days before)
    - Entry threshold: +0.10 (harder to enter)
    - Stop loss: ×1.8 (wider)
    - Position size: ×0.6 (smaller)
    - Max hold: ÷2 (close before announcement)
    - Take profit: tighter
  
  EARNINGS_WEEK (3-7 days before)
    - Entry threshold: +0.05
    - Stop loss: ×1.5
    - Position size: ×0.8
    - Max hold: ÷1.5
  
  NEGATIVE_CATALYST (material bad news detected)
    - Entry threshold: +0.15
    - Stop loss: ×2.0
    - Position size: ×0.5
    - Skip entries entirely
    - Close existing positions
  
  BULLISH_CATALYST (material good news detected)
    - Entry threshold: -0.05 (easier to enter)
    - Stop loss: ×0.8 (tighter, let winners run)
    - Position size: ×1.2 (larger)
    - Momentum weight boost: +0.10

Implementation:
  1. Create event_manager.py with EventRegime enum
  2. Define EVENT_MULTIPLIERS dict
  3. Update rank_candidates() to detect events
  4. Apply multipliers before any risk calculation
  5. Remove news/earnings from strategies
